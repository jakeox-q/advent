Data map: what the wireframes need from the data

For Jake and Ben. Every metafield in the import sheet (and on the dev store) mapped to where it displays, plus the gaps the layouts exposed. Built against the Enterprise theme by Clean Canvas, section names checked against its live demo.

Theme: Enterprise ✓

Built for big catalogues: scoped search bar, sidebar filters with counts, mega menu with promos, promo tiles in grid, quick buy, pre-order, back-in-stock, stock counter, Product details section with stat highlights + spec tabs. Approx $420 USD (client cost). Fallback: Horizon (free, currently live on the store), weaker on big-catalogue browsing.

Biggest data asks

1. Reverse expansion list on base games. 2. related_game as a list + filterable text mirror. 3. RRP decision. 4. Supplier lead time. 5. Product notice field.

What's already right

Derived bands for filtering, metaobjects for designers / mechanics / categories, state tags kept separate from merchandising, closed product type list. These map cleanly onto native filters.

Ben's script sets that order by bgg.rank nightly (unranked last) via the collection reorder API, and the theme labels it 'BGG rank'. Filters keep the order. Limits: one custom order per collection, so rating and release date can't also be sorts; search results stay relevance-sorted; Shop All needs a real 'All games' collection; new products sit at the end until the next run.

Add rating and rank bands if Dean wants those as filters. Free-value ranges need a filter app.

6. Questions for Dean for the next design review
  1. RRP: show strike-through 'was RRP' on everything, or a quiet RRP line on the product page with strike-through only for real sales?
  2. Preorders: charge at checkout or at release? Mixed orders held and shipped together by default?
  3. Is supplier lead time roughly the same for every supplier (one setting) or does it vary (per product)?
  4. Home hero: happy with fixed promo tiles instead of a rotating slideshow?
  5. Which 6-8 game-type tiles and which product carousels on the home page (Just Arrived, New to Preorder, Best Sellers, plus?)
  6. Range filters snap to bands (e.g. weight 2.0-2.9) rather than free values. OK, or worth a filter app?
  7. BGG rank as the default sort on collections, ahead of Newest? Rating and rank as filters too?
  8. Wishlist + back-in-stock app preference (one app can do both).
  9. Pickup: which Chatswood venue and time is current? (Pick Up page says Chatswood RSL 6-9pm, Shipping page says The Chatswood Club 6:30-9pm.) Is PlayCon a regular pickup point?
  10. Supplier dispatch time: Shipping page says 3-5 business days, FAQ says 2-7. Which do we show?
  11. Preorder ETAs: policy says dates appear 6-8 weeks before arrival, FAQ says 2-3 months. Which?
  12. Payments: keep bank transfer? Add Afterpay / Zip or Shop Pay instalments (not offered today)?
  13. 'At supplier' is shown as In Stock on the current site. Happy to show it as its own state with a 3-5 day promise?
  14. When was Advent founded? (2013 is the footer copyright year.)
