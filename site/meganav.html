Navigation + drawers

Every top-level menu item shown open, plus the search, cart and mobile drawers. Each panel uses one of Enterprise's three built-in mega menu layouts (Column, Button, Sidebar), picked to suit the size of that menu. Hover opens a panel, clicking the top-level label goes to its parent collection.

Menu itemEnterprise layoutWhy
PreordersColumn + promo bannersTwo short link groups plus room for timely promos (due this month, Kickstarter).
Just ArrivedButtonFive equal destinations, each better as a picture than a word.
Board GamesSidebarThe big catalogue: several groups with 8+ links each. Sidebar shows one group at a time instead of a wall of links.
RoleplayingColumnRPG customers shop by system first. Systems grouped by genre, plus an all-RPGs link, beginner shortcuts and accessories, so the menu covers the whole category.
AccessoriesColumn + promo bannersThree small groups by job (protect, upgrade, play) plus sleeve finder and organiser promos.
SaleStandard dropdownFour links. Kept small on purpose.
BrandsPlain link100+ brands go to the A-Z page.
All three layouts are Enterprise header blocks: add a mega menu block, pick its layout and the top-level menu item it attaches to. Links come from the Shopify navigation menu; images, badges and promo banners are set in the block. Any menu item without a mega menu block falls back to Enterprise's standard dropdown.

Enterprise Sidebar mega menu. Left list = second-level menu items with a thumbnail and chevron; hovering one swaps the image tiles on the right to its children. Types map to bgg.type, players to bgg.supports_players, awards to one Award winners collection filtered by award (tags set by Ben's script from the old category IDs), LCG and miniatures lines to bgg.families, the rest to smart rules (rank, weight) or tags, so every tile has real products behind it. Tile badges are set per tile in the block. Tile lists are placeholders: Dean to confirm which lines and awards to feature.

Enterprise Column mega menu. Follows how Australian RPG retailers organise the category (Good Games, Gameology, Games Empire all lead with game system, Good Games groups systems by genre). Systems are the main way in, with "All roleplaying games" first in the Browse column so nothing in the range is hidden, beginner shortcuts across every system, and RPG accessories. "Shop all" under each column goes to the full Roleplaying collection with that genre or kind filtered. System names here are placeholders: the real list comes from Dean's range (rpg.system values, ordered by sales), and genre comes from the rpg_system metaobject so a new system lands in the right column.
