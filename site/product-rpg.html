Delivery & pickup–

We ship Australia-wide with Australia Post eParcel, tracked. Games are boxed and padded; small items may go in a satchel.

  • $10 Sydney Metro, Gosford and Wollongong
  • $15 rest of Australia
  • Free on orders over $300

In-stock games are dispatched in 1-3 business days. Games we order in from our supplier ship in 3-5 business days.

Free pickup: Tuesday nights at Northside Gamers Group, Chatswood. Orders placed on a Monday or Tuesday are ready the following Tuesday.

We currently ship within Australia only. For overseas delivery, email us for a quote.

Confirm with Dean: supplier dispatch (site says 3-5 and 2-7 days in different places), pickup venue and time (Chatswood RSL 6-9pm vs The Chatswood Club 6:30-9pm).

Returns & damaged games–

Change of mind: let us know in writing within 7 days of delivery. The game must be unopened and in its original packaging. We offer an exchange or store credit. Preorders, special orders, sleeves and accessories can't be returned for change of mind.

Arrived damaged? Please note any damage when you receive the parcel, then email us for a Return Product Number (RPN). We'll replace the item, or give you a credit or refund for it.
